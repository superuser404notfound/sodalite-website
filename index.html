For Jellyfin and Seerr

Your Jellyfin, at its best.

Sodalite plays your Jellyfin library on Apple TV, iPhone, iPad and Mac. Native, fast, and in the picture and sound quality your files were made in. Request what's missing through Seerr, without leaving the app.

Free. One download for all your Apple devices.

Want new versions early? Join the TestFlight beta.

See it on every screen

One app with the same library, the same look and the same player, from the TV in the living room to the phone in your hand.

Everything on your server, and more

Sodalite talks to your Jellyfin server directly. Here is what you get.

Home that fits you

Continue Watching, Next Up, the latest additions, favorites, collections and playlists. Show, hide and reorder every row. Run several servers? Home can combine up to five into one.

Detail pages worth reading

Artwork first, the plot on the first screen, ratings from TMDB, IMDb and Rotten Tomatoes, format badges like 4K, Dolby Vision and Atmos, the cast with full filmographies, and every version of a title to choose from.

A player made for the remote

Frame-exact previews while you scrub, Skip Intro and Skip Recap, the next episode with a countdown, fast forward up to 256x, Picture in Picture, and subtitles in every format, including styled ASS and Blu-ray PGS. Search and download missing subtitles, or show two at once.

Live TV and recordings

A real program guide with favorites and categories. Pause and rewind live TV, switch channels with the remote, record a show or a whole series, and watch recordings while they are still running.

Request what's missing

Browse trending and upcoming movies and shows through Seerr. Request a movie, a whole series or single seasons, follow the status, and approve requests if you are an admin. One sign-in for both.

Multiview

Watch up to four live channels at once on Apple TV. The sound follows the tile you select, a click makes it full screen.

On the go

On iPhone and iPad: download movies and whole seasons to watch offline, send a title to a TV with AirPlay, or connect an HDMI adapter. Rotation lock and a child lock sit right in the player.

Native on the Mac

A real Mac app, not a port: a sidebar, the player in its own window, full keyboard control, full screen and right-click menus.

Your music, too

Browse your Jellyfin music by album and play it with a proper Now Playing screen, cover art, scrubbing and playback in the background.

Trakt built in

Connect Trakt with a code. What you watch is scrobbled, watched status and ratings sync both ways, and your watchlist and lists become Home rows. No server plugin needed.

Make it yours

Sodalite has a setting for almost everything, and each profile keeps its own.

Colors and backgrounds

Pick an accent color and a Graphite Glass or OLED Black background. Choose card sizes, artwork style, title logos and which badges you want to see.

Tab bar or sidebar

On Apple TV, navigate from the bar at the top or from a sidebar in your accent color. Choose which tabs appear, and pin a library like Movies as a tab of its own.

Your Home rows

Turn rows on and off, put them in your order, and add Recently Released, Upcoming episodes, your favorite episodes or a Latest row per library.

Your player buttons

Hide the player buttons you never use and put the rest in your order. Set the skip interval for each direction, and switch on Stats for Nerds when you want the details.

Sound for every room

Dialogue Boost makes voices clearer, Night Mode keeps loud scenes down at night, and a speaker setting folds surround to stereo or 5.1.

Subtitles your way

Font, size, position and style. Forced subtitles show on their own, and your track choice is remembered for each film and series.

Profiles and parental controls

Switch profiles without a password, each with its own settings. A Guardian PIN locks profiles and settings, and spoiler protection blurs episodes you have not seen yet.

iCloud Sync

Servers, sign-ins and settings follow you to every device on your iCloud account, end-to-end encrypted.

Powered by AetherEngine

Picture and sound, as they were mastered.

Underneath Sodalite runs AetherEngine, our own open source video engine. It plays your files as they are, so your server does not have to convert them, and it keeps what other apps throw away.

Plays nearly everything

HEVC, H.264, AV1, VP9, MPEG-2, VC-1 and old DivX and WMV files, in MKV, MP4, AVI, TS and more, even DVD and Blu-ray folders. Played directly, without transcoding on your server.

Real HDR

HDR10, HDR10+, Dolby Vision and HLG, with the full metadata. Apple TV switches your TV to the matching mode on its own, and Enhanced Upscaling sharpens SD and 720p video.

Dolby Vision Profile 7, too

Blu-ray remuxes with Dolby Vision Profile 7, FEL included, are converted on the device to Profile 8.1, so your TV still shows Dolby Vision. For FEL, the brightness of the enhancement layer is folded in scene by scene, which brings the picture much closer to the disc.

Dolby Atmos

Dolby Atmos from Dolby Digital Plus tracks lights up the Atmos sign on your receiver, and plays as Spatial Audio on AirPods.

Lossless height channels

Apple TV cannot pass TrueHD or DTS:X on to your receiver, so their height channels usually get lost. AetherEngine keeps them: it renders the track into a 9.1.6 mix and sends it to your receiver over HDMI without lossy compression.

Every subtitle

Text and image subtitles are drawn on the device: SRT, styled ASS, WebVTT, Blu-ray PGS, DVD, DVB and Teletext. Your server does not have to burn them in.
